## What you told us

You liked the sample page, and now you want it working for the bakery rather than sitting in a folder: live on your own web address, so people who search for you land on something that looks like Crumb & Co. And the buns sell out before noon, so regulars want a way to claim tomorrow's cardamom buns tonight and collect them in the morning, without phoning. You don't want to depend on anyone for small changes: you'd like to update the menu yourself, about twice a year.

## What changes for you

- Customers searching for Crumb & Co. find a fast, mobile-friendly page with your hours, address and menu highlights.
- Regulars can reserve next-day cardamom buns online and collect them in the shop, so fewer disappointed people at 11am.
- You can change menu items and prices yourself in minutes, without calling us.

## Scope — included

1. **Site launch.** Take the approved one-page design live on your domain, with HTTPS, working on phones and desktops.
2. **Pre-order form (pick-up only, next day).** Customer enters name, phone or email, quantity and pick-up day. Orders arrive to you as an email. Includes a cut-off time for next-day orders and a daily maximum quantity you set.
3. **Self-editable menu.** The menu section is driven by one simple file or form you can edit, with a one-page written guide.
4. **Handover.** A 30-minute walkthrough and the written guide.

## Not included (please read)

- **Online payment.** Pre-orders are reservations; customers pay in the shop. Taking payment online is a separate, larger project.
- **Delivery,** or any pick-up other than next-day at 12 Harbour Lane.
- **Stock or order management** beyond the daily cap: no dashboard, no customer accounts, no reminder texts.
- **Domain purchase and hosting fees.** These are billed to you directly by the provider.
- **Copywriting and photography** beyond the text already in the sample page.
- **Ongoing changes** after handover, other than fixing defects in what we delivered.
- **Menu updates on your behalf** (you'll do these yourself; we can quote separately if wanted).
